How to use Predictopia

Short guide for pool members and admins. No tech jargon — just how to play and run your league.

Quick start

  1. Sign in with Google.
  2. Join a pool via the link someone shared, or create a pool and share yours.
  3. On match days, open Predict, tap a team before the deadline.
  4. After lock, see everyone’s picks and climb the leaderboard.

Predictions lock about 30 minutes before the match start (before toss). Change your mind as often as you like until then — only the last pick counts.

For members

Joining a pool

  • Open the invite link → sign in if needed.
  • Free pool: you join immediately.
  • Paid pool: request to join → wait for admin approval → pay offline (UPI/cash) → admin marks you as paid.

Making predictions

  • Tap the team you think will win (no draw option for IPL).
  • You can predict upcoming matches in advance (next few fixtures).
  • After the deadline, picks lock and other members’ picks are revealed.
  • Missed a match? You get 0 points for that match.

Leaderboards & streaks

  • See weekly / monthly / season standings inside your pool.
  • Streaks and badges (e.g. hot streak, perfect week) show on your profile.
  • You can be in multiple pools — predictions are separate for each pool.

For admins (pool creators)

Create a pool

  • Set name, buy-in (or ₹0 free), commission, prize split style.
  • Copy the permanent join link and pin it in your WhatsApp group once.

Day-to-day

  • Approve join requests; mark members Paid when money arrives (separate from approve).
  • Share leaderboard / “who hasn’t predicted” cards back to the group.
  • Results usually auto-load; override only if something’s wrong.

Predictopia does not move money. It only tracks buy-ins, prizes, and who has been paid. Payouts stay offline.

Tips & FAQ

When does my pick lock?

At the match cutoff time shown on the card — roughly half an hour before start. After that you can’t change it.

Why can’t I see others’ picks yet?

Picks stay private until cutoff so nobody copies. After lock, the reveal grid appears.

Can I be in more than one pool?

Yes. Each pool has its own predictions and leaderboard.
