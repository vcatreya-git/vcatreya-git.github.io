How to use Predictopia

Short guide to groups, pools, predictions, and settlement tracking. Written for people who play — not for engineers.

Quick start

1
Sign in with GoogleThat’s your account — no password to remember.
2
Join a groupOpen the invite link → request to join → wait for a group admin to admit you.
3
Opt into a poolA pool is one tournament (e.g. this IPL or this Premier League). Tap Join — you’re not auto-enrolled.
4
Predict before lockOpen Predict, pick a winner, change anytime until the countdown ends.

Cutoff is shown on each match. Cricket locks ~30 minutes before start; football depends on the competition (often 60–90 minutes). Times always show in your local timezone.

Groups & joining

What’s a group?

Your lasting circle of people — family, office friends, cousins. Created once. Future tournaments don’t need a new invite list.

Join flow

  • Open the group link → sign in if needed.
  • You land as pending until a group admin admits you.
  • Denied? You stay locked out of that group.
  • Once in, you see pools under the group as “not joined” until you opt in.

Who creates pools?

Only a group admin. Pools set buy-in and prize rules, so the same people who vet membership create seasons.

Playing a pool

Predictions

  • Cricket: pick Team A or Team B.
  • Football: Home, Draw, or Away.
  • Miss a match → 0 points for that match.
  • In more than one pool for the same competition? You can sync picks or set them separately.

Specials

Admin-created polls (e.g. “who makes the final?”). Answer before their deadline if one is set. Same scoring area as matches once resolved.

After lock (Reveal)

Others’ picks stay private until cutoff. On the Board, open the Reveal section to see who picked what — the banter moment.

The four tabs (every pool)

TabWhat it’s for
PredictOpen picks and specials — what you need to do now
BoardStandings (filter by period), members, Reveal after lock
MeYour points and why each point landed
MoneyWhat you’re owed / settled, plus full winners list
ManageAdmins only — economics, specials, buy-in collected

Home cards show a next action: Predict if anything is open, else Money if you’re owed, else wait.

For admins

Group admin

  • Share the permanent group invite link (e.g. pin in WhatsApp).
  • Admit or deny join requests.
  • Add co-admins; create pools under the group.
  • Removing someone from the group drops them from its pools.

Pool admin / Manage

  • Create a pool (sport, competition, buy-in, fee %, prize splits, periods).
  • Mark buy-in Collected / Not collected when money arrives offline.
  • Push and resolve specials; unlock late entries if needed.
  • Close a period on the Money tab — freezes winners for that window.
  • Mark prizes Owed / Settled after you pay people offline.

Predictopia never moves money. It only tracks buy-ins and who is owed or settled — like Splitwise for the pool. Cash stays UPI / cash outside the app.

Prizes

  • Admin sets per-period and season pots, and how ranks split (e.g. 50/30/20).
  • Closing a period writes who won what. Result fixes after close don’t rewrite that pot.
  • Special prizes settle when the special is resolved (exact winners).

FAQ

Why can’t I see others’ picks?

They stay private until that match’s cutoff so nobody copies. After lock, use Board → Reveal.

Can I be in several pools?

Yes. Each pool has its own picks, board, and money.

Do I need to re-invite everyone next season?

No. Same group; admin creates a new pool and people opt in.

How do I take a guided tour?

Open the prototype and tap ? Tour above the phone — pick Member or Admin. You can reopen it anytime the same way.

Is this live with real data?

The public site includes a full interactive prototype. Production backend is still in build.
