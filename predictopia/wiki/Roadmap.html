Roadmap — Predictopia

The product vision across phases: what capability each phase unlocks, who benefits, and what must be true before it ships. Companion to Feature Map and Decision Log.

This page showcases sequencing discipline: V1 proves admin labour → 0; growth features wait for habit.

Product evolution milestones

V1: Can the family run a full season without Sheets?

Capability: Create pool, permanent invite, one-tap predictions, cutoff lock + reveal, schedule + results, scoring, leaderboards, streaks, achievements, commission prize engine, offline payment tracking, share cards.

Who it serves: Family organisers (admin) and casual predictors who live in WhatsApp.

Validates: Does admin labour collapse after setup? Do seniors complete a pick without help? Does the pool survive past the first two weeks of IPL?

Dependencies: None — this is the foundation. Status: implemented in code (2026-03); production re-deploy + family E2E remaining.

In scope (shipped in code):
- Google auth, onboarding, favourite team tint
- Pool create, permanent join link, multi-admin (WhatsApp model)
- Join request + paid states
- One-tap predictions, advance picks, cutoff lock, reveal
- Schedule seed + cron refresh, on-demand results
- Scoring 2/0/1, leaderboards, streaks, achievements
- Commission prize engine, budget tracker
- Share cards / QR
- PWA shell

Out of scope (intentional):
- WhatsApp/Telegram Business APIs
- In-app payments / KYC
- Public pool discovery
- Phone OTP
- Upset Caller (needs underdog data)

V1.1: Can money and ops feel less rough after real use?

Capability: Member “My Money” (winnings only), better unpaid nudges, bulk paid mark, stronger result reliability, timezone polish, Upset Caller if data exists.

Who it serves: Same family after first season feedback.

Validates: Which friction is real vs imagined? Does money UX reduce admin DMs?

Must be true before shipping:
- At least one full family season (or partial season with real UPI flow)
- List of top 5 friction points ranked by admin time / member confusion

Sequencing rationale: Polishing money UX without real payments is guessing. V1.1 is data-driven, not calendar-driven.

V2: Can this grow beyond one family without breaking the product?

Capability: Multi-league templates beyond IPL, opt-in reminders (email/Telegram), discoverable public pools (is_public already in schema), optional comments on reveal if families want social.

Who it serves: Organisers who want second/third pools; non-family groups (college, office).

Validates: Does public discovery create support/abuse load? Do notifications increase prediction completion without spam?

Must be true before shipping:
- V1 retention signal (members return match-day without admin nagging)
- Abuse and pool-moderation plan for public pools
- Notification opt-in design (family hate spam)

Why not earlier: Distribution without habit is empty growth. V1 private invite is the correct wedge.

High-level release table

Phase User promise Gate When
V1 (built) Run a full IPL family league without Sheets Pool + predict + score + prizes Code complete 2026-03
V1.1 Money UX + nicer ops After first family season feedback Fast follow
V2 Growth beyond one family After V1 retention signal Post-season

Sequencing logic

V1 proves admin labour → 0
  → V1.1 polishes money & ops from real friction
  → V2 expands distribution only after habit exists

No “maybe features” floating unowned. Deferred items live in V1.1/V2 with a reason — portfolio evidence of honest scope.

What success looks like by phase
