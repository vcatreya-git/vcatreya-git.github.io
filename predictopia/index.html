How it works

Three layers: people, tournament, and predictions.

1

Group

Persistent circle of people. Join via invite link; admins admit or deny. Survives every future season.

2

Pool

One competition under a group (e.g. IPL or Premier League). Members opt in — no auto-enroll. Admin sets buy-in, periods, and prizes (tracking only).

3

Predict

One-tap outcomes before cutoff. Admin specials (polls) use the same event model. Scores and boards update when results resolve.

4

Money tab

Who won what, what you’re owed, settled or not — like Splitwise for the pool. Cash moves offline (UPI / cash).

In the app

Pool navigation is four tabs for everyone, plus Manage for admins.

Predict

Today’s open picks and specials. Cutoff countdown. Sync across pools that share a competition.

Board

Standings with period filter. Members list. Reveal of others’ picks after lock (collapsible).

Me

Your points and per-event score breakdown — why each point landed.

Money

Your owed / settled first, then full winners. Close period lives here for admins.

Manage admin only

Economics, prize split, buy-in collected, push / resolve specials. Never mixed into the Board.

Who it’s for

Group admin

Owns the circle, admits members, creates pools, stays trusted for money rules.

Pool admin

Runs the season — specials, period close, settlement toggles.

Member

Predicts, checks the board, sees what they’re owed. Senior-friendly targets and copy.
