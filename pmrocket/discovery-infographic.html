Validated Problem Statement

Aspiring Product Managers — engineers, MBAs, consultants, analysts, designers — cannot navigate the PM career transition because the preparation landscape is fragmented, generic, and disconnected from what employers actually screen for.

Every existing platform delivers content. None tells a candidate which PM role suits their background, what specific portfolio evidence to build for that archetype, or when they are genuinely ready to apply.

The result is a 6–18 month preparation loop without external calibration, while employer data confirms that 40% of rejections happen at the domain expertise filter — the exact gap no platform addresses.

The Structural Gap

Every existing platform delivers content. None of them deliver navigation.

No platform tells a candidate which PM archetype suits their background, which companies are realistic targets, what specific evidence to build, and when they are genuinely ready to apply. The demand side makes the absence even more acute: hiring managers screen on domain depth and business impact evidence — the exact things no prep platform helps candidates build in a targeted way.

2

2026 layoff wave intensifies competition

55,875 tech cuts YTD. Experienced PMs flooding a market with aspiring PMs. Application-to-offer compressed to ~4%.

3

Demand bifurcating by seniority

Senior PM demand +87% YoY in India. Entry-level roles −15% YoY globally. Fewer spots, higher bar.

4

Domain depth is the primary hiring filter

40% rejected at screen without domain experience. Generalism no longer clears the first gate anywhere.

5

What employers test has changed fundamentally

Portfolio evidence, SQL fluency, business outcome articulation. Framework memorisation is dead.

6

AI PM is the highest-growth, highest-bar segment

300% posting growth. $280K–$492K US total comp at senior level. Specific technical bar: eval sets, model lifecycle.

7

Internal transition path (28% of hires) is unserved

Employer-preferred, lower risk, faster to productivity. No platform exists for this use case.

8

The gap is navigation, not content

Every platform delivers content. None tell a specific candidate which role to target, what to build, or when they're ready.

9

WTP is validated — but category-dependent

Truth Seekers (6–18 months in market, still failing) have highest WTP. Already spent ₹27K–₹150K. Will pay for signal over content.

10

No kill conditions triggered

Problem real, gap unoccupied, market growing, urgency highest in the 2021–2026 window. No reason to stop.
