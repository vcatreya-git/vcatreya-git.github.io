What this app is

PMRocket helps aspiring PMs stop guessing. It answers three questions:

  • Which PM path fits me? (archetype match)
  • What should I build as proof? (personalised roadmap)
  • How ready am I? (evidence + readiness signal)

It is a navigation tool — not another pile of random courses. Progress only counts when you log real evidence.

Your path through the app

1
Onboarding Short intro to how PMRocket works. You can skip if you’re ready.
2
Intake (about 5–7 minutes) Answer questions on background, goals, skills, and experience. Answers save as you go — you can leave and resume.
3
Match See your primary PM archetype (e.g. Technical PM), match confidence, and domain focus. You can explore other archetypes later from the menu.
4
Dashboard (Home) Your home base: progress, next recommended artefact, and shortcuts into the roadmap.
5
Roadmap → log evidence → Readiness Work artefacts layer by layer, log what you completed, watch readiness update.

Roadmap & evidence

Three layers

  • Universal — skills every modern PM needs (including AI literacy)
  • Archetype — craft for your path (e.g. Technical PM)
  • Domain × market — e.g. fintech in North America

Logging evidence

  • Open an artefact → mark topics / completion level → save.
  • Link a portfolio piece or notes when you have them.
  • Deeper completion (e.g. Practised / Mastered) strengthens your readiness more than a light pass.

If you’re unsure what to do next: open Home and follow the “next step” artefact, or filter the roadmap by your weakest skill track.

Readiness & scores

  • Readiness reflects how much of your roadmap you’ve evidenced — not a judgment of your worth as a PM.
  • Match % is how well your intake answers fit an archetype under our scoring model — not a hiring guarantee.
  • Gap analysis (when available) explains gaps using market-style signals and points you back to artefacts.

Later features (apply pack, interview practice, coach) will build on the same evidence you’ve already logged.

Tips & FAQ

Can I retake the assessment?

Yes — menu → Retake assessment. Your logged evidence is kept, but your match and roadmap may change.

Why does the roadmap look empty?

Finish intake and match first. Content is seeded for supported archetype × domain slices; unseeded combos may show less.

How often should I log evidence?

Small, regular logs beat rare marathons. Aim for a weekly rhythm if you’re actively prepping.
