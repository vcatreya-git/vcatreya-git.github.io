User Flows — PMRocket

1. Hero Flow — Wanderer (Aspiring PM)

Goal: Turn "lost in preparation" → "confident I'm ready to apply"

Step What the user sees What they do What happens
1 PMRocket landing page: "Know your PM archetype. Build your evidence. Know when you're ready." Clicks "Get started" or "Sign up with Google" Redirected to Clerk auth (email or OAuth)
2 Onboarding splash: "15 questions. 3 minutes. Your navigation starts here." Clicks "Begin intake" Lands on Q1
3 Q1–Q4 (Background): Single-select questions. Role history, education, geographic location. Feels like a career assessment. Answers each question (1 answer per Q). Can go back. State saved to user_intake row after each answer. Green checkmark appears.
4 Q5–Q11 (Domain signals): Company size, stage, domain interest, timeline to apply, location preference, salary expectations, PM function focus. Single-select answers. Questions build up a profile in real time. "Your interests: B2B SaaS, Series B–C stage, North America" summary appears.
5 Q12–Q14 (Skills): "Rate your technical depth 1–10," "Rate your product thinking 1–10," "Rate your business acumen 1–10." Skill grid with 1–5 radio buttons. Completes grids. Skills profile surfaces: "Your strength: technical foundation. Area to develop: business acumen."
6 Q15 (Activities): Multi-select checklist. "Which PM prep activities have you done? Customer interviews, case studies, shipped features, competitive analysis, metrics frameworks..." Checks 5–7 activities. Checklist saves. Summary: "You've built foundation in {areas}."
7 "Computing your match..." Loading state with 2-second animation. Waits. System runs computeArchetypeMatch() against 15-signal weighted model.
8 Match Result Hero Card: "You're a Technical PM (78% confidence). Your backup: Growth PM (64%)." Breakdown shows which signals were strongest (technical depth scored highest; domain signals nudged toward B2B). "Your evidence roadmap is waiting—log in to see it." Teaser: "You have 24 artefacts to build. Get started to unlock your roadmap." Clicks "See my roadmap" or "Sign in" Free users hit paywall. Paid/invited users proceed to Evidence Roadmap.
9 Paywall screen (if not subscribed): Pricing card ($5/mo or invite code). "Full roadmap + evidence tracking + readiness score + gap analysis. See what you need to build." Monthly or annual billing. Enters card details OR pastes invite code. Stripe checkout flow (test mode in Alpha). Subscription row created in subscriptions table. Redirects to Evidence Roadmap.
10 Evidence Roadmap: Three tabs (Technical, Business, Portfolio, Domain, Product). 24 artefacts listed. "Customer interview study," "shipped feature," "strategy memo," "competitive analysis." Each shows: title, layer badge (Universal / Archetype / Domain), Learning/Practised/Mastered progress bar. "Search to learn" keyword chips (clickable → Google search). Clicks an artefact card. Evidence Log Modal opens.
11 Evidence Log Modal: "Log {artefact name}." Shows 15 key_topics split into 3 tiers. Learning tier shows 5 topics as checkboxes: "Customer pain identification," "Segmentation logic," "Interview script prep," "Data collection," "Documentation." User checks off 2 topics. Optional description field (200 chars): "Did 8 interviews with fintech users re: ACH payments." Link field: "https://docs.google.com/..." Checks 2 Learning topics. Adds description + link. Clicks "Save evidence." POST /api/evidence-log fires. Score recalculation triggered synchronously. User sees "Score updated! +7 pts." Tier unlock animation if Learning → Practised triggered. Returns to Roadmap.
12 Dashboard: Grid shows Readiness 23/100, Evidence logged 1/24, Archetype Technical PM (78%). Below: "Next artefact: Metrics framework design. 3 people logged this this week." Gamification strip: "3-day streak! Milestone: 50% readiness (22 pts to go)." "Gap analysis not yet available (seeding JD corpus for your slice)." Coming-soon teasers: "Ready-to-apply gate (V2a)" and "Interview practice (V3)." User explores roadmap or clicks "Analyse my gaps." Roadmap re-renders filtered. Gap button shows placeholder (not yet live for this slice).
13 (Loop) User logs evidence regularly. Dashboard updates each time. After 12+ artefacts logged and readiness ≥75 (V2a+), gap analysis becomes available. Clicks "Analyse my gaps." RAG gap analysis triggered (K=25 JD retrieval + LLM narrative). Returns top 3 skill gaps + recommended artefacts to close them fastest.

Key moments:
- Match result is the free → paid conversion point. User sees their archetype and a teaser roadmap size, then is asked to subscribe to see the full roadmap.
- Evidence log is the core return-visit mechanic. User returns to log artefacts, watch score climb, and unlock tiers.
- Gap analysis is on-demand, not automatic. User clicks "Analyse" → runs RAG → receives narrative + ready-to-apply gate.

2. Navigator Flow — Existing PM (V2a.1)

Goal: Transition from one PM role / domain / archetype to another using market-grounded gap analysis.

Branching at Q16: After Q15, intake shows "Are you currently a PM?" If No → continue as Wanderer. If Yes:

Step What they see What they do What happens
Q16 (Branch) "Are you currently a PM?" (Yes / No) Selects Yes Proceeds to Q17 (Navigator branch)
Q17 "What's your current PM level? IC1 / IC2 / IC3 / IC4 / Staff / Director / VP+" Selects "IC2 (Senior PM)" Signal saved. Match algorithm gives less weight to background (Q1–Q4) because they already know their PM type.
Q18 "What's your primary transition goal?" Multi-select: Level up / Switch domain / Switch archetype / Move geography / Expand skills Selects "Switch domain (fintech → B2B SaaS) + Expand skills" Branching logic activated. Q19 shown (multi-select skills).
Q19 (conditional) "Which skill areas do you want to focus on? (select up to 3)" Options: Monetisation frameworks, enterprise sales cycles, integrations complexity, data infrastructure... Selects "Monetisation" and "Enterprise sales cycles" Skill-area signals inform roadmap filtering downstream.
Match result Same as Wanderer, but archetype weights adjusted. E.g., if Growth PM but Q18 = "Switch to Enterprise PM," Enterprise signals get boost. Sees match result. Proceeds to Evidence Roadmap (paid).
Evidence Roadmap Same 3-layer structure, but filtered. Layer 1 (Universal) shown fully. Layer 2 and Layer 3 filtered to Q19 skill areas and Q18 transition goal. "You're skipping Engagement metrics (not your focus) but need Customer success models (transition-relevant)." Logs evidence as usual. Score computation adjusted: Navigator users get credit for existing PM experience (can skip some foundational artefacts).
Gap analysis Roadmap filtered to gaps only. LLM narrative includes "You know X already (IC2 fintech PM). The gap: Y (B2B SaaS Enterprise skills)." JD retrieval uses seniority_level filter. Only Senior PM JDs retrieved (not entry-level). Clicks "Analyse gaps." Gap narrative is role-transition-specific: "Your Enterprise PM gap: multi-year contract negotiation, deployment complexity, customer success ops. Try these artefacts: {X, Y, Z}."

Key differences from Wanderer:
- Background signals (Q1–Q4) carry less weight — they already know their PM type.
- Q17 (level) and Q18 (goal) carry more weight in match algorithm.
- Roadmap is filtered, not full. Transition-relevant artefacts only.
- Gap analysis retrieves JDs at their target seniority level (e.g., Senior PM JDs, not IC1).
- Score computation recognises existing PM experience; doesn't force re-learning foundational artefacts.

3. Admin Editorial Flow (V2a)

Goal: Keep curriculum market-grounded without code deploys.

Step What they see What they do What happens
1 Admin ETL panel: "Paste a job description." Large text area. Pastes raw JD text from LinkedIn, company career page, or email. JD saved to temporary pending_extractions row.
2 Metadata form: "Domain (B2B SaaS / Fintech / Consumer)?" "Geography (North America / India)?" "Seniority (IC1 / IC2 / Staff / Director)?" "Archetype hint (Technical / Growth / Enterprise)?" Selects metadata tags. Tags attached to JD.
3 Button: "Extract skills." System calls POST /api/extract-skills. Waits. LLM extracts skills from JD (PII scrubbed). Output limited to existing skill_frequency.skill_name vocabulary (no new skills invented).
4 Preview panel: Extracted skills shown as checkboxes. "Stakeholder management," "Roadmap prioritisation," "SQL query analysis," etc. Each with a confidence label (High, Medium). Admin can uncheck skills to reject them. Reviews skills. Unchecks any that seem wrong (e.g., "SQL query analysis" is probably dev, not PM). Checkbox state captured in UI.
5 Button: "Approve selected." Clicks button. POST /api/confirm-extraction fires. LLM narrative generated. JD embedded via text-embedding-3-small (1536 dims). Embedding written to jd_embeddings table. Skill frequencies updated: skill_frequency rows incremented for the selected skills (domain + geography slice).
6 Curriculum Signals panel (auto-updated): Three tabs: Trending / Stable / Declining. Trending shows "Multi-year contracts (appeared in 4 recent JDs, not yet in curriculum)" with "Add to curriculum" button. Declining shows "Agile ceremonies (was in curriculum 6 months ago, now in 2/last-20 JDs)" with "Archive" button. Clicks "Add to curriculum" for trending "Multi-year contracts" skill. Modal: "Create artefact: Multi-year contract negotiation. Layer (Universal / Archetype / Domain)? Archetype (Enterprise PM only). Key topics?" Admin fills in 15 key topics.
7 Admin dashboard shows JD ingestion velocity: "12 JDs this week, 180 skills extracted, 2 new artefacts created, 3 declining skills archived." Periodically reviews trends. Curriculum stays aligned with real hiring patterns without manual research.

Key principle: Nothing updates the live curriculum without explicit admin approval. The LLM extracts and suggests — the human decides. Vocabulary constraint prevents skill name fragmentation.

4. Evidence Logging Loop (Core retention mechanic)

Goal: User logs artefact → score updates → dashboard shows progress → motivation to log next one.

Step Time What happens
1 T+0 User opens Dashboard. Sees gap_pct and "Next artefact: Metrics framework design."
2 T+10s Clicks "Roadmap" tab. Sees 24 artefacts. Filters to "Business" category. Clicks "Metrics framework design" card.
3 T+20s Evidence Log Modal opens. Shows 15 key_topics split into 3 tiers. Learning tier shows 5 topics. User checks 3 of 5 (e.g., "Framework selection," "Key metrics selection," "Dashboard design").
4 T+30s User types description: "Built a metrics dashboard for fintech app. Tracked onboarding funnel, churn, and revenue per user."
5 T+40s User pastes link: "https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/abc123"
6 T+45s Clicks "Save evidence." Optimistic UI update: card shows 3/5 topics checked. Button disables briefly.
7 T+50s POST /api/evidence-log completes. Score recomputed synchronously: Technical artefacts already have 3, now +1 = 4. Points = 4 × 7 (Business artefact) × 0.5 (Learning tier) = 14 pts. Total score now 37/100 (was 23). Tier unlock check: Learning 3/5 = 60% → Practised 0/5 = 0%. Practised not yet unlocked.
8 T+51s Celebration animation: card glows green. "Score updated! +14 pts. Learning tier: 3/5 (60%). Practised tier locked until Learning complete." User sees new total: 37/100. Streak increments to 4-day.
9 T+55s Modal closes. Roadmap re-renders. "Metrics framework design" card now shows Learning progress (3/5) with "Practised tier locked" hint.
10 T+60s User clicks another artefact. OR closes app. OR clicks Dashboard to see new score.

This loop is the product's core retention mechanic. Each evidence log is a micro-win: visible progress, score bump, tier unlock animation, streak continuation.

5. Upskill Flow (Navigator + Q19)

Goal: User targeting skill area doesn't see full roadmap—sees focused curriculum for their goal.

Step What they see What they do What happens
1 (Q18) "What's your primary transition goal?" Multi-select options. Selects "Expand skills" (not "Level up" or "Switch domain"). Q19 shown next.
2 (Q19) "Which skill areas? (select up to 3)" Options: Customer research, monetisation frameworks, enterprise sales, data infrastructure, design thinking, AI/LLM product thinking. Selects "Monetisation frameworks" and "Data infrastructure." Skill-area tags saved to user_intake.Q19_skills.
3 (Match) Match proceeds normally. Receives archetype match. Proceeds to Evidence Roadmap.
4 (Roadmap) Evidence Roadmap filtered. Layer 1 (Universal) shown fully. Layer 2 and 3 filtered: only artefacts tagged with "monetisation" or "data" skill areas shown. "Hiding: 8 artefacts outside your focus areas. View full roadmap?" Sees focused list. ~12 artefacts instead of 24. Feels less overwhelming. Evidence logging proceeds normally.
5 (Gap) Gap analysis available but positioned as secondary. Primary navigation is filtered curriculum ("Your next artefact: CAC/LTV frameworks"). Optionally clicks "Analyse gaps" for broader view. RAG pulls JDs, but narrative is skill-area-filtered: "Top gaps in monetisation: churn analysis, expansion revenue calculation, pricing strategies. Try these artefacts: {X, Y}."

The upskill path is a focused learning menu, not a gap-closing checklist. It removes decision fatigue and keeps the user on track for their transition goal.
