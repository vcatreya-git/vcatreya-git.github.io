Roadmap — PMRocket

The product vision across phases: what capability each phase unlocks, who benefits, and what must be true before it ships.

Product evolution milestones

Alpha: Can you navigate?

Capability: Match your archetype. See your personalised roadmap. Log evidence. Track readiness.

Who it serves: Aspiring PMs who are directionless but can start building evidence.

Validates: Does the navigation insight work? Do users engage with evidence logging? Does the readiness signal feel credible?

Dependencies: None—this is the foundation.

V2a: Can you know your gaps?

Capability: Gap analysis tied to real job descriptions. Understand where you stand against the market, not against invented rubrics.

Who it serves: Users with 6+ weeks of evidence logged, ready for honest feedback.

Validates: Does market-grounded intelligence change user behaviour? Do gaps + recommendations drive more evidence building?

Must be true before shipping:
- Sufficient JD corpus (80+ JDs for primary demo slices)
- Matching quality proven by Alpha signal
- Admin ETL pipeline stable (paste → extract → review → embed)

Sequencing rationale: Intelligence unlocks the Apply layer. You can't draft an honest resume without knowing the gaps. You can't practice interviews without knowing what matters.

V2a.1: Can existing PMs navigate too?

Capability: Navigator path for existing PMs. Same product, different intake (Q16–Q19 ask about current level and goal). Gap analysis filtered to target level, not from zero.

Who it serves: PMs who've shipped before but want to level up, switch domain, or move to a new market.

Validates: Does the product work for experienced PMs as a career progression tool?

Must be true before shipping: V2a corpus and gap pipeline stable. Navigator branching logic low-risk (reuses existing intake/match/roadmap infrastructure).

V2b: Can you stay engaged?

Capability: Readiness history (chart showing gap trajectory). Email nudges (weekly next artefact recommendation). Shareable portfolio page (public proof URL for interviews).

Who it serves: Users who completed Alpha or V2a. Retention hooks for ongoing engagement.

Validates: Do history and nudges keep users coming back? Does portfolio page become a job application asset?

Must be true before shipping: Real usage and retention data from Alpha or V2a. This phase is data-driven, not calendar-driven.

V3a: Can you apply with proof?

Capability: Paste a job description. Get a resume draft built entirely from your logged evidence, re-framed for that specific role.

Who it serves: Users ready to apply (gap ≤ 30%). Need honest packaging of their proof.

Validates: Does evidence-backed resume generation increase application success rate? Do users trust the honesty of the output?

Must be true before shipping:
- Gap analysis proven accurate
- Evidence log quality stable
- Users have shipped to 3+ archetypes/domains so resume generation has signal

Why here, not earlier: Applying is the outcome, not the journey. Rushing users to apply before they have evidence is harmful. Apply tools make sense only after the navigation and intelligence layers validate.

V3b: Can you practice and discover?

Capability: Interview room (AI-driven mock interviews with feedback). Job matching (RAG over corpus to find open roles aligned with your evidence level).

Who it serves: Users who've drafted resumes and are close to applying.

Validates: Does practice improve interview outcomes? Does job matching help users find aligned opportunities?

Must be true before shipping: V3a (resume builder) live. Practice feedback should reference how you'd present your resume.

V3c: Can you stay on track?

Capability: In-app coach chat grounded in your roadmap and gap. Telegram micro-doses (spaced repetition for PM concepts at your chosen frequency).

Who it serves: Users in the job search and early career phases.

Validates: Does personalised coaching improve retention? Do micro-doses drive skill consolidation?

Must be true before shipping: Core product (navigation + intelligence + apply) proven. Channels without value delivery are overhead.

V4: The network phase

Capability: Job board curated by archetype and domain. Recruiter directory. B2B institutional features (for bootcamps, universities, companies).

Who it serves: A mature community of users. Companies looking to hire specialised PMs.

Validates: Can PMRocket become a career marketplace, not just a prep tool?

Must be true before shipping: 5K+ active users, $K/month ARR. The network only works at scale.

Why this sequencing?

Evidence is the spine. Every phase builds on the assumption that evidence is truth. You can't analyse gaps without evidence. You can't package a resume without gaps. You can't practice interviews without evidence to reference.

Intelligence before Apply. You won't build honest apply tools until you know the gaps. Rushing users to apply before they understand what they're missing is harmful.

Channels after value. Don't add conversational chrome (coaching, Telegram) before the core navigation and apply flows prove they work. Channels are delivery mechanisms, not the product.

B2C before B2B. The product is positioned for individual career progression, not institutional workflow. B2B (bootcamps, companies) only makes sense once B2C is proven and the network has formed.

Deferred (non-goals)

Feature Why
Interview marketplace Saturated space; PMRocket is navigation, not community
Employer dashboard B2C-first positioning; B2B only if demand emerges
Mobile native app Web + PWA sufficient; native is a luxury, not a blocker
LinkedIn/Indeed scraping Legal/ToS risk, anti-bot cost; paste + career pages first
Resume keyword theatre Violates evidence-as-truth principle
Auto-credit implied skills Breaks honest readiness signal
